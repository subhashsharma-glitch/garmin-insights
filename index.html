Your Garmin data, made clear.

Drop in your Garmin export and get an interactive read of your fitness, recovery and running, with charts across your whole history.

Private by design: your files are read and analysed entirely in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere: no server, no account, no cookies, no analytics. Close the tab and it's gone.

How to get your Garmin export
  1. Full history: Garmin Account Management Center → Data Management → Export Your Data. Garmin emails a ZIP; drop it here as is.
  2. Single workouts: Garmin Connect → activity → ⚙ → Export Original (FIT) or Export to GPX.
  3. Activity list: Garmin Connect → Activities → Export CSV.
