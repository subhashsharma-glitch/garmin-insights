How to get your Garmin export
  1. Full history: sign in at Garmin's Account Management Center → Data Management → Export Your Data → Request Data Export. Garmin emails a ZIP link (it can take a few days). Drop that ZIP here as is.
  2. Single workouts: in Garmin Connect open an activity → ⚙ → Export Original (FIT) or Export to GPX.
  3. Activity list: Garmin Connect → Activities → Export CSV (metric units).
